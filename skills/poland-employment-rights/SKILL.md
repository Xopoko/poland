# Poland Employment Rights

Use for employment or civil-law contract questions, unpaid or late pay, working
time, leave, termination, workplace safety, employer abuse, and State Labour
Inspection routes. Permission to work belongs to `poland-work-authorization`.

Confirm worker or employer perspective, contract type in the user's words,
workplace locality, event and deadline, requested remedy, and whether danger,
coercion, withheld documents, or immigration consequences are present. Do not
request names, employer identifiers, payslip values, or unredacted records in
plugin tools.

Use current PIP and competent official sources. Build an evidence timeline and
separate contract rights, safety, complaint jurisdiction, tax/ZUS questions,
and residence consequences. Draft questions or a placeholder complaint outline
for review; never send allegations, resign, accept a settlement, or predict a
legal result.

Route urgent exploitation or danger to `poland-emergency-rights`, stay impact to
`poland-stay-residence`, tax to `poland-tax`, and insurance to
`poland-social-insurance`. Read `../../references/work-tax-business.md`.
