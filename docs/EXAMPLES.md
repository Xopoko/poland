# Example Requests

These examples are intentionally non-identifying. Adapt categories, not personal
identifiers.

## Moving and First Weeks

> Build a first-weeks Poland checklist for an EU citizen moving for work. Keep
> the steps compositional, explain which office owns each step, and flag anything
> that depends on locality or current portal behavior.

## Residence and Work

> Compare the candidate residence routes for a third-country worker without
> deciding eligibility. Separate applicant, employer, and voivode responsibilities
> and cite the official source for every material claim.

## Documents

> Explain whether apostille, legalization, sworn translation, or recognition
> could be relevant to a foreign diploma. Ask only category-level questions and
> leave the result undetermined when the destination authority is unknown.

## Digital Government

> Explain the roles of Trusted Profile, ePUAP, mObywatel, e-Tax Office, and eZUS.
> Give me public navigation only and stop before login.

## Driving

> Explain the difference between the six-month recognition period and the
> 185-day residence condition mentioned for a driving-licence exchange. Do not
> assume either clock applies to me.

## Delay or Refusal

> Help me classify this situation at a high level: ordinary status check,
> supplementation request, complaint about inactivity, refusal review, or
> appeal. Do not ask for the decision, case number, or correspondence.

## Source Freshness

> Show the official sources behind this checklist, their access dates and
> freshness status as of today. If a dynamic condition is stale, do not fill the
> gap from memory.
