# Emergency and Escalation

## Immediate danger

Show `emergency-112` prominently when life, health, safety, or property is in immediate danger. The user places the call or report. Do not delay emergency guidance to complete intake or research.

If speaking or device use may be monitored, minimize visible detail and do not create a durable narrative without the user's request.

## Exploitation or trafficking indicators

Use `anti-trafficking-kcik` when the user reports coercion, threats, withheld identity documents, forced work, sexual exploitation, forced begging, or inability to leave. Present the official contact and available language support. Do not contact an employer, recruiter, landlord, or suspected perpetrator.

## Legal escalation

Use `free-legal-aid` when there is an adverse decision, appeal or objection, disputed status, tenancy conflict, near deadline, or need for individual legal interpretation. The plugin may prepare a chronology and evidence list but must not represent the user.

Use `solvit` only for a plausible cross-border EU-rights problem involving a public authority. It is not a general complaint channel.

## Data minimization

Do not place emergency narratives, identity numbers, exact safe addresses,
medical details, third-party allegations, credentials, call details, or location
shares in bundled plugin tools or durable artifacts. Ask only what changes
immediate safety. When a caller-owned tool is already being used for a requested
non-emergency administrative follow-up, explicit task scope may permit minimum
necessary on-screen details under `automation-playbook.md`; this never justifies
delaying emergency help or collecting unrelated sensitive history.

## Public handoff evidence

For the public evidence record, return only the official source ID, observation
time, contact purpose, and published language. Do not persist what the user chose,
whether contact occurred, or whether help was received. The user places emergency
calls and controls emergency location sharing. A non-emergency message or booking
may be assisted only under task scope and fresh action-time confirmation; never
claim it occurred without a visible receipt.
