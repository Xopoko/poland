# Poland Emergency and Rights Escalation

Use when there may be immediate danger, violence, medical emergency, trafficking,
exploitation, detention/removal, withheld documents, homelessness, a missing
person, or an imminent legal deadline. Do not bury urgent action inside a normal
administrative checklist.

## First response

1. If immediate danger is possible, tell the person to use Poland's emergency
   number 112 or seek nearby human help. Do not auto-call, impersonate them, or
   delay action while collecting details.
2. Ask only what changes immediate safety: whether they are safe now, whether
   emergency services are needed, approximate locality, and what communication is
   safe. Do not demand immigration status or documents first.
3. Surface the relevant official specialist route from the offline emergency
   sources: police, medical help, trafficking support, labour inspection, free
   legal aid, crisis intervention, or consular help.
4. For a time-critical letter or status issue, preserve the deadline and obtain
   qualified legal help; do not improvise an appeal.

The plugin never diagnoses, dispatches, contacts a service, guarantees external
confidentiality, or promises a legal result. Do not request or persist a
sensitive narrative. Read `../../references/emergency-and-escalation.md`, which
is designed to be usable offline; verify non-emergency public contact details
when time permits.
