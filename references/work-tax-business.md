# Work, Tax, and Business

## Work

Start with status and work context, not the contract label alone. Use:

- `biznes-legal-work` for current official orientation on work by foreign nationals;
- `praca-gov-pl` for public employment-service forms and channels;
- `pip-employees` for labour rights, safety, and complaint routing.

Do not conclude that work is permitted, that a document is sufficient, or that an
employer is compliant. A complaint or employer message may be prepared in a
caller-owned tool after explicit task scope and sent only after a fresh visible
summary and action-time confirmation. Resignation, settlement, signature, and
legal attestation remain user-controlled. Coercion, threats, withheld documents,
or unsafe work route immediately to `anti-trafficking-kcik` or `emergency-112`
as appropriate; do not delay safety for automation.

## Tax

Use `podatki-home` for public guidance. For `e-tax-office`, research the public
instructions first, then use the protected-service contract in
`automation-playbook.md` when hands-on help is requested. The user authenticates,
signs or attests, and completes final payment authorization. Explicit task scope
permits minimum relevant notice or record inspection and form filling; filing,
correction, payment initiation, upload, or download requires action-time
confirmation. Do not choose a tax treatment or accept a declaration's truth for
the user.

Appointments through `tax-office-appointments` consume a scarce slot. Verify the
public office and topic. After user-only authentication, task-scoped form filling
is allowed; booking, rescheduling, or cancellation requires a fresh summary of
the office, service, time, and consequence plus action-time confirmation.

## Social insurance

Use `zus-home` for public information. For `ezus`, the user authenticates and
explicitly scopes any account-record access. A caller-owned tool may inspect the
minimum relevant record and fill necessary fields. Booking, filing, sending,
payment initiation, upload, download, or record change requires action-time
confirmation; signatures, attestations, and final payment authorization remain
with the user. Do not treat tax registration, business registration, health
insurance, and social-insurance enrolment as interchangeable.

## Business

Use `biznes-ceidg` to distinguish public search from registration or record changes. Use only allowlisted, unauthenticated public access to `krs-search` and `gus-regon-api` for official registry evidence. Collect the proposed legal-form category, current-status category, and intended effect, not identifier values.

Never select a legal form, tax method, insurance title, or accounting treatment as an automated outcome. Route those choices to a qualified human adviser when they are material.

Registry or account operation follows `automation-playbook.md`. Account creation,
registration, upload, download, payment initiation, or record change is
confirmation-gated; signature, legal/tax attestation, irreversible closure, and
final bank authorization are user-controlled.
