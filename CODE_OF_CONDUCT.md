# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes respectful, evidence-focused collaboration from people of
all backgrounds. Discuss ideas and public artifacts, not a contributor's
identity, nationality, immigration status, language level, or personal case.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, pressure to disclose
personal records, and publication of private data are not accepted. Keep
feedback specific, actionable, and proportionate to the change under review.

Report conduct or security-sensitive concerns through the repository's private
security channel when public discussion could expose a person or vulnerability.
Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation
to protect contributors and the public-data boundary.
